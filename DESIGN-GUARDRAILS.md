# AuraGlobal Design & Conversion Guardrails

These rules are mandatory for every new page and every future redesign.

## Absolute no-gos
- No unreadable text. Important copy must never rely on low opacity, tiny type or weak contrast.
- No clipped or hidden comparison data on mobile.
- No overlapping cards, badges, buttons, labels or text.
- No dead links, empty category links or placeholder CTAs.
- No irrelevant imagery. Every photo must support the product, use case, ingredient, category or buying decision.
- No irrelevant video. Every video must explain the product, use case, category or buying decision.
- No fake ratings, fake reviews, fake urgency, fake prices or invented product facts.
- No generic brand page that simply repeats the merchant shop.
- No large brand dump on the homepage as the platform scales.
- No expired deal presented as current.
- No important information hidden behind hover-only interactions.
- No affiliate-tech wording in visible customer copy except the required disclosure/Anzeige note.

## Readability
- Desktop body/supporting copy: target >= 14px.
- Mobile decision/supporting copy: target >= 12px.
- Mobile technical data / metadata: target >= 10px.
- Primary comparison values must be visually stronger than labels.
- Dark backgrounds require high-contrast copy; muted text must still be immediately readable.
- Light backgrounds require dark copy with sufficient contrast.
- Comparison cards and tables must be full-width or horizontally scrollable on small screens.
- Never compress three technical comparison columns into unreadable phone tiles.

## Homepage architecture
The homepage must scale to hundreds or thousands of partners:
1. Emotional hero + search
2. Current verified deals / strongest savings
3. Category entry points
4. Buying-decision value proposition
5. Popular comparison journeys
6. Visual/video inspiration
7. Link to complete brand/comparison directory

Individual brands should only dominate the homepage when there is a strong current reason: a verified deal, launch, exclusive code, exceptional buying relevance or editorial feature.

## AuraGlobal value
Every merchant page must answer:
- Why should the user visit AuraGlobal instead of going directly to the merchant?
- Which differences actually change the buying decision?
- Which product fits which use case?
- What does it cost per useful unit/portion/use when relevant?
- Is there a currently verified deal or better-value bundle?
- What should the user check before buying?

## Deals
- Only publish deals verified from the merchant or affiliate program.
- Show a checked/updated date.
- Show end date when known.
- Remove expired offers promptly.
- Do not invent discount percentages.
- Prefer specific useful claims: "20% off", "€200 off", "4 sleeves free from 20" over vague "sale".

## Permanent visual requirement — Thomas, 10.10.2026
- Keep photos and manufacturer/product videos prominent on the homepage in every conversion pass.
- Place the video inspiration rail immediately after the hero; do not bury it behind a long stack of offers or quizzes.
- On mobile, offer photos must be a full-width visual stage, never a narrow thumbnail strip.
- Keep purchase routes alongside strong visuals. Take inspiration from leading companies' product presentation principles while preserving AuraGlobal branding, truthful claims and original assets.
- Verify real video embeds and images remain in published markup after reordering; do not rely solely on JavaScript relocation or autoplay.

## Emotional presentation — Thomas, 10.10.2026
Emotion is the highest-priority presentation principle: vivid everyday moments, strong relevant imagery and video, inviting copy and clear purchase paths. Keep facts accurate and terms readable. Do not display "Stumm" badges or autoplay implementation explanations on customer-facing video cards.

## Audible stories — clarification from Thomas, 10.10.2026
Removing the Stumm label alone is insufficient: videos must offer audible playback to stimulate both eyes and ears. Preserve prominent sound activation and usable player controls. Browser restrictions may require a user gesture for sound; do not promise automatic audible playback on first visit. Only one clip plays with sound at a time.
